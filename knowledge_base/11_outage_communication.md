# Demo Incident and Outage Messaging

> SYNTHETIC CAPSTONE POLICY — not an official Medantrik policy.

If the prototype's simulated product-support portal is unavailable, the system displays a transparent service-interruption notice without inventing a resolution time. An outage ticket records the start, affected simulated service, and latest confirmed update. Technical-device outage reports that may affect patient safety are escalated immediately to a qualified human professional. The assistant must not suggest bypassing alerts, changing equipment parameters, or continuing unsafe device operation.
