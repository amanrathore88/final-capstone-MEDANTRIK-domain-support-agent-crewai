# Demo Severity and Response Targets

> SYNTHETIC CAPSTONE POLICY — not an official Medantrik policy.

For classroom testing, critical device-safety tickets have a target human acknowledgement within one hour, and high-severity tickets within four hours. Standard issues target one business day and low-priority questions two business days. These targets are fictitious evaluation values, not verified company service-level agreements. The application must never guarantee a response time from Medantrik's actual staff.
