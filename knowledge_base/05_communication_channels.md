# Demo Communication Channels

> SYNTHETIC CAPSTONE POLICY — not an official Medantrik policy.

The prototype provides an in-app support chat and simulated ticket-status tracking. Medantrik's publicly listed general enquiries email is info@medantrik.com, but this demo does not send messages to that address automatically. Synthetic ticket notifications can be shown within the application only. Do not imply a live staffed support channel or actual emailed reply.
