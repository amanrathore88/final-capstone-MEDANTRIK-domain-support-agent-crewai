# Demo Refund and Compensation Handling

> SYNTHETIC CAPSTONE POLICY — not an official Medantrik policy.

A refund or compensation request is only recorded as a ticket in this demonstration. The assistant cannot promise eligibility, a repayment date, a refund amount, or change a payment record. A human reviewer must inspect the synthetic order context and decide whether the request can proceed. No real Medantrik refund policy or transaction processing is included.
