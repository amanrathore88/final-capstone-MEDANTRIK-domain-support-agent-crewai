# Demo Ticket-Priority Policy

> SYNTHETIC CAPSTONE POLICY — not an official Medantrik policy.

In this synthetic capstone, product-safety concerns and equipment alarms receive critical priority and immediate human escalation. Repeated outages, inaccessible accounts, and unresolved technical problems receive high priority after triage. Routine product information, demo enquiries, and training requests normally receive standard priority. Ticket priority is a demonstration workflow rule, not a published Medantrik policy.
