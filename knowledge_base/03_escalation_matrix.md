# Demo Escalation Matrix

> SYNTHETIC CAPSTONE POLICY — not an official Medantrik policy.

Escalate safety-related OxySense, NODex or Iron Lung reports to a simulated biomedical-device specialist queue without providing procedural device settings. Escalate billing disputes to human billing review and account-recovery problems to a verified support operator. Uncertain product claims and unsupported knowledge-base answers also require human review. Store the selected destination and escalation reason in the audit trail.
