# Demo Ticket Retention and Privacy

> SYNTHETIC CAPSTONE POLICY — not an official Medantrik policy.

The prototype stores fabricated tickets and support chat events in a local SQLite database for reproducible evaluation. A fictional ninety-day retention setting can be used to demonstrate privacy controls, but the app must not claim that this is Medantrik's official retention policy. Input guards mask Indian phone number patterns before logging or passing data to agents. Do not request or retain patient clinical records, real order details, or actual customer medical identifiers.
