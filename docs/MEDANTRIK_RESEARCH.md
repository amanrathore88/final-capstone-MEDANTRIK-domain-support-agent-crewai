## Medical-safety boundary
- This is a **support & triage** app, not a clinical diagnostic device. No diagnosis, treatment advice, prescription adjustment, oxygen delivery changes, calibration procedures, bypassing alarms, or interpreting a patient's results.
- On an active medical emergency / possible unsafe equipment behavior: recommend immediate local emergency/clinical assistance according to local procedures and make a critical human ticket; do not say an agent is currently online unless that is verified.
- Do not collect patient identifiers or medical files in the capstone. Use fictitious customers and synthetic tickets only.
